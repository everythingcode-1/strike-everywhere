TSLA lifts guidance as traders rotate back into risk

Markets desk · 20 October 2026 · 4 min read

TSLA shares rose in early trading after the company raised its delivery outlook for the fourth quarter. Analysts at two banks lifted their price targets, citing better margins and a stabilising order book.

The move was not isolated. NVDA added 1.4%, while CRCL and AAOI followed the broader bid for high-beta names. In crypto, $BTC traded near its weekly high and $ETH reclaimed the 2,400 level.
