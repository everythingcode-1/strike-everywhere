# Design direction

## Read

A product landing page for traders and research readers, in an editorial-terminal visual language. The page first feels like a reading surface, then reveals the terminal-like market card. Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

## Palette

- Graphite `#0a0a09` is the product surface. The extension is a dark overlay on third-party articles, so a dark landing page keeps the brand world continuous.
- Ivory `#f4f1e8` carries text. Warm paper `#efe9dc` marks sections about reading, the claim flow, and the model notes.
- Mint `#36f5ae` is the single accent: primary actions, the ticker underline, live values, and the card edge.
- Danger red and warning amber appear only in product captures or in the risk limitation note.

## Type

- Instrument Sans for interface and headlines. It has a tighter, more editorial voice than the default shadcn stack.
- Newsreader for the sample article. The serif makes the reading context visible rather than described.
- IBM Plex Mono for market values, commands, and timestamps. Prices and receipts are data, not prose.

## Composition

- Hero: a large editorial headline beside a sample reading view and a real extension capture. The card overlaps the article because the product overlays the page.
- The reading surface section is warm paper. It separates the article world from the terminal world and uses a large screenshot as evidence.
- Ghost Trades stays dark and uses a four-step evidence chain. Two real side-panel captures carry the proof.
- Risk replay returns to paper so the 43,200 figure reads like an editorial statistic. The dark stress-test panel is the object on the page.
- Privacy is a wide data-boundary ledger, not a row of identical cards.
- Install is a copyable command with three concrete steps. No Chrome Web Store button exists because no listing exists.
- FAQ uses native disclosures. The answer text is in the HTML, not hidden behind a client-only accordion.

## Motion

MOTION 1. The hero words settle once on first view. The rest is hover and focus. There are no endless pulses, floating cards, or scroll choreography.

## Identity motif

The dotted mint underline on ticker symbols. It comes from the extension and repeats in the hero, the ticker strip, and the sample article.

## Rules

- No fabricated market numbers, customers, ratings, or download counts.
- No claim that this is an official Strike product. The footer says it is an independent hackathon build.
- Every link points to a section that exists or a real external site.
- Every product image is a real capture; synthetic demo values are labelled.
