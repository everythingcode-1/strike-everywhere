## Accounting regressions

- A 0.1 BTC short entered at $50,000, 10x leverage, $500 cash, and 0.5% MMR has a model threshold of $54,726.36815920 before entry fees. At that price equity and maintenance both equal approximately $27.36318408. The old $44,776.11940299 output did not satisfy that identity.
- Long and short thresholds are checked with an independent equity-minus-maintenance residual; a tier-crossing short exercises the tier at the candidate mark.
- Entry fees reduce available margin. Hourly funding uses remaining signed quantity and the candle open at UTC settlement times; the opening instant is excluded.
- Slippage is not cancelled by rescaling the mark path. A mark of 100, execution entry of 105, and historical mark low of 92 liquidates the tested 10x long; scaling the path to execution entry had incorrectly reported survival.
- Worst adverse excursion stops at the first liquidation event and retains precision until display. Partial reductions continue under the documented one-attempt-per-minute model.
- Exactly 43,200 consecutive completed mark minutes are required. Missing, duplicated, stale, malformed, non-finite, and non-positive data cannot produce a 30-day verdict.

These regressions were first observed failing before the corresponding fixes were applied. They validate the stated model, not equivalence to Strike's entire exchange engine.

## UI direction and antislop review

The approved direction preserves a compact dark trading panel for desktop readers. Market price leads the compact card; scenario evidence leads the expanded section. System fonts fit a 320px surface without loading external assets. Blue is reserved for the primary action and focus; red and green carry labelled adverse/favourable states. The card shadow separates an overlay from the article; internal sections stay flat. Motion is limited to the existing panel transition and respects reduced-motion settings. Dial: energy 1, rhythm 2, motion 1.

- Hard gate PASS for the changed UI: browser assertions cover states, keyboard, focus return, actions, and narrow overflow; contrast is measured; the UI contains no fabricated adoption, performance, or safety claims. Synthetic evidence is labelled.
- Purpose gate PASS: compact numerical layout serves the trading task; the single overlay elevation, system typography, restrained action colour, and limited motion have the purposes above. No remote visual assets, decorative gradients, or icon library was added.
- Liveliness PASS: price and scenario verdict form distinct focal points, spacing separates controls from evidence and assumptions, and the repeated ticker-to-risk interaction identifies the product. The expanded result is not an unrelated dashboard.
- Craftsmanship PASS for the exercised flows: every changed action was clicked or keyboard-triggered, source and network are labelled, unsupported isolated margin is refused, and replay assumptions are visible next to the estimate. Three real third-party websites and a logged-in Strike order ticket were not exercised in this run.

## Reproduce browser checks

Use an existing Playwright installation and Chromium binary. The paths below are arguments rather than project dependencies:

```powershell
node scripts/verify-browser.mjs "C:/path/to/playwright/index.mjs" "C:/path/to/chrome.exe"
npm run build
node scripts/verify-unpacked.mjs "C:/path/to/playwright/index.mjs" "C:/path/to/chrome.exe"
```

The first script serves local fixtures on port 4187 and blocks external fixture requests. The second serves the existing demo on port 4188, launches a clean extension profile, and uses the public testnet API. Both close their browsers and servers. Clean verification profiles remain in the ignored `.superpowers/unpacked-profile-*/` directories.
